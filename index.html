All Coil Grades, One Address

Hot rolled to colour coated — flat steel coils in every grade, width, thickness and finish, made to Indian and international standards.

Hot Rolled Coils (HR)

1.2–25.4 mm, up to 2000 mm wide. IS 2062 E250–E410, ASTM A36, SAE 1008–1541 for structures, tubes, auto chassis and LPG cylinders.

Cold Rolled Coils (CR)

0.3–3.0 mm close-tolerance sheet. IS 513 O/D/DD/EDD, IF grades for deep drawing, panels, appliances and precision tubes.

Galvanized & Galvalume

Zinc (Z60–Z275) and zinc-aluminium (AZ70–AZ150) coatings for corrosion-proof roofing, cladding and construction.

Colour Coated (PPGI / PPGL)
