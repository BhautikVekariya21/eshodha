The Full Journey

16 Stages of Integrated Coil Making

Steps advance automatically — or click any stage. Stage 1 begins in the raw-material yard and Stage 16 ends on a dispatch rakes bound for your plant.

STAGE 01 / 16
Upstream · Captive Supply

Raw Material Sourcing & Handling

Iron ore from our captive mines in Karnataka and Odisha arrives by rail along with imported coking coal, dolomite, limestone and manganese ore. Automated sampling towers verify chemistry at the gate; stacker-reclaimers blend ore into homogeneous beds so the furnace never sees a surprise.

STAGE 02 / 16
Ironmaking Feed

Sinter Plant

Fine ore, fluxes and return fines are fused into porous sinter on our 312 m² sinter machine. Sinter gives the blast furnace a high-reducibility feed and lets us recycle plant waste dust back into metal — the first circular loop in the chain.

1.2 MTPASinter Capacity
312 m²Sinter Machine Area
< 40 mg/Nm³Stack Emissions
STAGE 03 / 16
Ironmaking Feed · Byproducts

Coke Ovens & Byproduct Recovery

Imported low-ash coking coal is carbonised at 1,100 °C in 52 recovery-type ovens. The coke charges the blast furnace; tar, benzol and coke-oven gas are captured for the chemical market and for reheating furnaces — nothing is flared.

0.55 MTPACoke Output
52 ovensBattery Capacity
~180 Nm³/tCoke Oven Gas Recovery
STAGE 04 / 16
Ironmaking

Blast Furnace — 1,500 t/day of Hot Metal

Sinter, coke and flux descend against a 1,150 °C wind-blast; oxygen-enriched combustion and coal injection (up to 180 kg/tHM) reduce ore to 4.3% carbon hot metal at 1,480 °C. Two 1,750 m³ furnaces feed the steel shop around the clock, with slag granulated for cement.

2 × 1,750 m³Furnace Volume
2.2 MTPAHot Metal
180 kg/tCoal Injection Rate
STAGE 05 / 16
Steelmaking

Basic Oxygen Furnace (BOF)

A water-cooled oxygen lance blows 99.6% pure O₂ through molten iron and scrap, burning carbon, silicon and phosphorus out in 16 minutes. Sub-lance sensors read carbon and temperature in-flight, so every heat lands in its specification window the first time.

2 × 120 tConverter Heats
1.9 MTPACrude Steel
±0.005%End-point Carbon Hit Rate
STAGE 06 / 16
Chemistry Control

Secondary Refining — LF, RH Degasser & CAS-OB

Grades are finalised here: the ladle furnace trims chemistry and temperature; the RH degasser pulls hydrogen below 1.5 ppm for auto and electrical steels; wire feeding shapes inclusions into harmless forms. Electrical and AHSS grades get double refining.

140 gradesRoutinely Refined
< 1.5 ppmDissolved Hydrogen

STAGE 07 / 16
Solidification

Continuous Slab Casting

Liquid steel flows through a submerged entry nozzle into water-cooled copper moulds, emerging as 200 mm × 900–1,900 mm slabs cut on the fly. Dynamic soft reduction and electromagnetic stirring lock in internal soundness; every slab gets a heat number laser-etched on its face.

1.6 MTPACaster Throughput
9.5 m/minCasting Speed
99.2%Slab Yield
STAGE 08 / 16
Rolling Prep

Slab Reheating Furnaces

Walking-beam furnaces soak slabs to 1,200–1,250 °C with oxygen-free firing to keep scale loss under 0.8%. Combustion air preheaters recover heat from flue gas, cutting furnace fuel by 22% versus cold-air baseline.

300 t/hrReheating Rate
±5 °CDischarge Uniformity
0.8%Scale Loss
STAGE 09 / 16
Hot Coils

1.7 m Hot Strip Mill

A descaler blast, four roughing passes and seven finishing stands roll the slab to 1.2–25.4 mm; laminar cooling sets the microstructure, and twin downcoilers wind coils up to 34 t. Hydraulic AGC holds gauge to ±0.05 mm, and laminar curtains customise cooling for dual-phase and pipeline grades.

2.8 MTPAHSM Capacity
18 m/sFinishing Speed
±25 °CCoiling Temp Control
STAGE 10 / 16
Surface Prep

Pickling & Descaling Line (CPL)

Hot rolled coils run through turbulence HCl pickling that strips mill scale without edge damage, then through dual-side oiling. Pickled coils (HRPO) ship directly to tube makers, or continue to the tandem mill. Acid is regenerated on-site — 98% closed loop.

1.6 MTPAPickling Capacity
98%Acid Regeneration
0 defectsOver-pickled Edges Target

Thin & Tight

Tandem Cold Rolling Mill

Five stands reduce the strip up to 85% in one pass at 1,350 m/min, with automatic flatness actuators and laser speed gauges. The result: 0.3 mm foil-gauge strip with gauge tolerance within ±0.02 mm and surface roughness engineered per customer dies.

1.35 MTPATCM Capacity
85%Max Reduction

Softening & Structure

Annealing & Temper Rolling

Hydrogen batch annealing and a continuous annealing line recrystallise the cold-worked grains to hit O/DD/EDD formability; skin-pass rolling then sets final flatness, roughness and yield-point behaviour so panels draw without stretcher strains.

Coating

Continuous Galvanizing & Galvalume Line

Strip passes degreasing, annealing and a zinc (or 55% Al-Zn) bath at 460 °C; nitrogen knives meter the coating from Z60 to Z275. Galvannealing furnaces produce GA for automotive; a temper mill and tension levelling guarantee roofing-grade flatness.

Finishing

Colour Coating & Painting Line

On the 300,000 TPA CCL, coil is pretreated, primed, painted (PE / SMP / PVDF) and cured in a 40 m oven — all under closed-loop VOC capture. Inline spectrophotometers match customer shade cards ΔE < 0.8, batch after batch.

Closed-Loop Operations

Every Byproduct Has a Second Job

Integration is not just forward to coils — it's sideways into energy, cement and chemicals.

Slag to Cement

Gas Recovery

5-stage effluent treatment; 94.8% process water recycled, zero liquid discharge certified.
